## 3. Level 2 Verification: New Application Onboarding (10-Step Lifecycle)

The onboarding of a brand-new application (`MASTERDB_TEST_CONSUMER`) was executed in 10 deterministic steps:

1. **Self-Identification:** Registered as `MASTERDB_TEST_CONSUMER` in domain `ENVIRONMENT`.
2. **Capability Discovery:** Queried TANTRA for `"Mangrove Monitoring Data"`. TANTRA resolved `cap.masterdb.mangrove_biomass_retrieval` targeting `DS-THANECREEK-2023-BIOMASS`.
3. **Semantic Understanding:** Resolved MDU schema `biomass.schema.json` (Units: `Mg/ha`, fields: `measured_agb_mg_per_ha`, `estimated_agb_mg_per_ha`).
4. **Purpose Declaration:** Submitted purpose `INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_VALIDATION`.
5. **Authorization:** Received cryptographically signed TANTRA access token with a 300-second TTL.
6. **Execution Invocation:** Dispatched execution call through TANTRA without direct database drivers.
7. **Data Retrieval:** Retrieved canonical record: Measured AGB = `84.83 Mg/ha`, Estimated AGB = `111.31 Mg/ha`.
8. **Provenance Verification:** Retrieved citation `DOI: 10.1016/j.ecolind.2023.109876` and publisher `ScienceDirect / Elsevier`.
9. **Telemetry Generation:** Emitted W3C traceparent to InsightFlow (`00-0b71fa139bd848bd8ec26dd31754e2ab-01e31c86f3b34983-01`).
